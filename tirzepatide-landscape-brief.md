## 1. Headline Trial: Phase 3b Tirzepatide vs. Semaglutide in Obesity (NCT05822830 / SURMOUNT-5)

| Field | Registry Value |
|---|---|
| **NCT ID** | NCT05822830 |
| **Acronym** | SURMOUNT-5 |
| **Official Title** | A Phase 3b, Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Compared to Semaglutide in Adults Who Have Obesity or Overweight With Weight Related Comorbidities |
| **Sponsor** | Eli Lilly and Company (industry) |
| **Phase** | Phase 3b (Phase 3b; randomized, parallel, open-label) |
| **Current Overall Status** | **Completed** (primary completion Nov 13, 2024; status verified Nov 2025) |
| **Enrollment** | **751 participants** (actual) |
| **Design** | Randomized (1:1), parallel assignment, no masking, treatment purpose |
| **Arms** | Tirzepatide up to 15 mg or MTD (SC QW, tirzepatide = LY3298176) vs. Semaglutide up to 2.4 mg or MTD (SC QW) |
| **Population** | Adults with BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with ≥1 weight-related comorbidity (hypertension, dyslipidemia, OSA, CVD); **without** type 2 diabetes |
| **Duration** | ~74 weeks (72-week treatment) |
| **Primary Endpoint** | Percent change from baseline in body weight (Baseline → Week 72) |
| **Results** | Posted to registry (first results posted Nov 26, 2025); primary completion + PMID 40353578 (NEJM 2025) |

**Key results at a glance:** LS mean percent change in body weight was **−20.2%** with tirzepatide vs. **−13.7%** with semaglutide (LS mean difference −6.5 percentage points, 95% CI −8.1 to −4.9). ≥10% weight reduction: **87.7%** vs. **66.7%**; ≥15%: **71.7%** vs. **45.0%**. All sites in the US; enrollment 374 tirzepatide / 376 semaglutide. Serious AEs: 18 (tirzepatide) vs. 13 (semaglutide).

**Takeaways:**

- Phase 2 is the largest discrete interventional bucket (80), feeding the Phase 3 engine.
- A meaningful share (~19%) of studies = N/A or Unknown phase — mostly non-phased observational work and post-marketing surveillance.

## 3. Quick Context Points for Discussion

- **SURMOUNT-5 (NCT05822830)** is the head-to-head against the semaglutide 2.4 mg obesity dose — Lilly's peri-oven weight-loss trial in non-T2D obesity with comorbidities.
- Trial status is no longer "recruiting" — it's **Completed with results posted**, so the team can discuss finished data rather than follow-on trials.
- Phase distribution shows a mature pipeline (strong Phase 3/4 footprint) but still a large Phase 2 arm for ongoing expansion into new populations (MASH, HFpEF, OSA, T2D).
- IPD sharing available (anonymized, per research proposal + DSA) — relevant if the journal club discusses reproducibility/transparency.
